# House rules (summary)

The full rules are shown by `tadam join` (and on the site); the person reads them and types the agreement
**themself**. You never accept them, never type «согласен» / "I agree", never run `tadam join` on their behalf.
A new version of the rules must be accepted again before pushing and voicing work.

1. **Only what the editor has the right to use.** Every picture, sound, voice, character and text: made by them,
   generated by them within the generator's terms, or licensed for this use. Say where every file comes from. No
   other people's music, no ripped art, no famous characters, no real people without their written consent.
2. **Clean content.** No swearing, insults, sexual or adult content, gore, hate or harassment, politics,
   propaganda, nothing illegal. TadamShow is for everyone from 16 up and is played by real people — write roles a
   stranger is glad to record.
3. **No spam, no abuse.** No floods of submissions, no misuse of the voice service or the storage, no probing of the
   service; the studio key stays with its owner.
4. **TadamShow decides what goes public.** Nothing is published without approval; the owner may edit, extend and
   continue a submitted world, crediting its author.
5. **At the editor's own cost.** Their AI keys and tools are theirs to pay for; editing is voluntary.

Breaking the rules means the content is removed and the account and email are banned.

What this means for you: suggest only original heroes and stories; keep texts clean in every language; generate
only with the person's yes and their key; never upload anything you are not sure the person may use — ask.
